Hi, I'm Lauren! 

I'm a PhD student at the [Centre for Astrochemical Studies](https://www.mpe.mpg.de/CAS) in Munich, supervised by Dr. Jaime Pineda. I work on star and planet formation, and I'm currently particularly excited about accretion streamers towards protostars and how we can model them to learn more about them. I want to answer what they're made of, where they come from, and how much of an impact they have on the forming disc.

I made a tool to quickly fit an analytical infall model to streamer emission. It's called [STING](https://github.com/Lauren4476/sting) - STreamer INfall with Gradients! Contributions and/or feedback welcome.

I'm currently working in [PRODIGE](https://noema-prodige.github.io/), a survey of 32 Class 0/I protostars in the Perseus molecular cloud, and 8 Class II protoplanetary disks in the Taurus molecular cloud.

Feel free to get in touch :)
